The Founder Behind the Vision

Meet Ashley Rodgers

RN, BSN

Founder of Leaux Line Wellness, registered nurse, holistic wellness practitioner, and student midwife.

A thoughtful approach to whole-person wellness

Ashley Rodgers is the founder of Leaux Line Wellness, a holistic wellness brand grounded in the belief that well-being deserves care, intention, and room for every part of the person. As a registered nurse and holistic wellness practitioner, Ashley brings together clinical experience, traditional wellness practices, and a deep commitment to helping people feel seen, supported, and connected throughout their wellness journeys.

Ashley’s love for community is at the heart of her work. She believes meaningful wellness grows through connection, access to education, shared experiences, and spaces where individuals and families feel welcomed and supported. This passion is especially present in her commitment to community-based maternal care. As a learning midwife studying community midwifery, Ashley is continuing to deepen her understanding of relationship-centered care and the role community plays in supporting families through pregnancy, birth, and the postpartum period.

Alongside her maternal health interests, Ashley values the ways movement, education, and holistic practices can support whole-person well-being. Yoga, childbirth education, Reiki, and restorative wellness experiences are part of the broader vision for Leaux Line Wellness. The brand welcomes individuals and families at different stages of life, with an approach rooted in respect, education, connection, and intentional care.

Professional Background

  • Registered Nurse (RN)
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
  • Certified Reiki Practitioner
  • Stretch Practitioner
  • Yoga
  • Childbirth Educator
  • Student Midwife — Da Roots Traditional Midwifery School (community midwifery)

Midwifery education is currently in progress. Ashley is a learning midwife and continues to build her knowledge and experience in community midwifery.

The Leaux Line Philosophy

Leaux Line Wellness brings a thoughtful, inclusive approach to holistic wellness and maternal health. The focus is on creating space for restoration, education, connection, and intentional care—honoring each person’s unique needs and wellness journey. Ashley’s commitment to community, yoga, and childbirth education reflects the belief that wellness is strengthened when people have access to knowledge, supportive relationships, and care that honors the whole person.
