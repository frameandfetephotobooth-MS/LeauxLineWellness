About Leaux Line Wellness

Founded by Ashley Rodgers, RN, BSN, Leaux Line Wellness blends clinical knowledge with holistic practices to support the whole person — mind, body, and birth.

Our Offerings

  • Yoga and stretching
  • Juicing and wellness education
